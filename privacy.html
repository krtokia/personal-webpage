Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how krtokia.dev ("we", "us") handles information in connection with this website and the personal software applications and tools operated under the krtokia.dev domain (collectively, the "Services").

1. Information We Collect

This website is a static informational page and does not itself collect personal information, use tracking cookies, or run analytics.

Some of our applications allow you to sign in with a third-party account (such as a Google Account). When you choose to do so, we may receive:

  • Basic profile information (such as your name, email address, and profile picture) provided by the sign-in provider.
  • Data you explicitly grant the application access to through the provider's consent screen (for example, specific Google services), limited to the scopes you approve.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Authenticate you and provide the core functionality of the application you are using.
  • Maintain and improve the operation of the Services.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share it with third parties, except as required to operate the Services (for example, hosting infrastructure) or as required by law.

3. Google User Data

Where an application accesses Google user data through Google APIs, its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Google user data is used only to provide or improve user-facing features of the application you authorized.
  • Google user data is not transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide those features, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with prior notice.
  • Google user data is never used for advertising.
  • Humans do not read Google user data unless you give explicit permission, it is necessary for security purposes, it is required by law, or the data has been aggregated and anonymized.

4. Data Storage and Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the Services. Authentication tokens are stored securely and are not shared. When data is no longer needed, it is deleted.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at the address below. You can also revoke an application's access to your Google Account at any time via Google Account permissions. Upon revocation or a deletion request, associated data is removed from our systems.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction, including encrypted transport (HTTPS) and access controls.

7. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or to make a data request, contact: krtokia@gmail.com
